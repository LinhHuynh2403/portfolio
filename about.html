About Me

I build AI systems that are trustworthy by design. I care less about getting a model to talk and more about the engineering around it — grounding, verification, and cost — so whether it's an agent that rejects any claim it can't trace to a verbatim source or a RAG system that cites its exact source and refuses to answer when it can't, the guarantees live in code, not just prompts. I'm "customer zero" for everything I ship: I use my own tools daily, hit the rough edges, and fix them.
